About this simulation

Practice version of the Error Decoder — Find Your Error, Fix It lab. It mimics the lab’s real outputs in your browser, so you can practise the commands on your phone. Nothing real runs or installs here.

The real lab: Errors are not failures — they are the computer telling you exactly what went wrong, in its own words. This practice terminal lets you cause four common errors on purpose, then fix each one and see it go away.

What it does NOT do: it does not run a live service, it does not hold real data, and it does not touch your machine beyond this tab.

Your proof: Save the four error lines and their fixes. Next time one appears in a real lab, you will know it by sight.

After you finish: run the real lab with Docker on your computer — the same commands, on your own machine. That is where the learning sticks.
