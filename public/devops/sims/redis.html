Two-way idea, in plain words: what if two stores both accept writes at the same time? Then they must merge the changes. That merge rule is called a CRDT. It is a paid feature. The free Redis in this lab does not have it. One-way copy is free and simple; two-way copy is a paid feature with rules you must understand first.
