About this simulation

Practice version of the Interview Pack — Real Questions, What They Really Test lab. It mimics the lab’s real outputs in your browser, so you can practise the commands on your phone. Nothing real runs or installs here.

The real lab: Interview questions for support jobs are really just broken things in disguise — they ask what you would type first. This practice terminal gives you four of those moments and lets you answer with a real command.

What it does NOT do: it does not run a live service, it does not hold real data, and it does not touch your machine beyond this tab.

Your proof: Save the four answers: the process list, the stopped container, the pod that has not restarted, and the promoted standby. Each one is the answer to a real interview question.

After you finish: run the real lab with Docker on your computer — the same commands, on your own machine. That is where the learning sticks.
