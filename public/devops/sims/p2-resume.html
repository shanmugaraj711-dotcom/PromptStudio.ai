About this simulation

Practice version of the Resume Bullets — From Your Projects, In Simple Words lab. It mimics the lab’s real outputs in your browser, so you can practise the commands on your phone. Nothing real runs or installs here.

The real lab: A resume bullet is only true if you can show the proof behind it. This practice terminal checks each bullet from the resume lesson with the command that proves it.

What it does NOT do: it does not run a live service, it does not hold real data, and it does not touch your machine beyond this tab.

Your proof: Save the five proofs: the site answering, the pipeline commit, the plan, the monitoring answer, and the two-member cluster. Each one backs a bullet on your resume.

After you finish: run the real lab with Docker on your computer — the same commands, on your own machine. That is where the learning sticks.
