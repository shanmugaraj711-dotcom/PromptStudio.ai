# Part 2 — The Fix Checklist

## The idea in one line

AI assistants recommend businesses they can *find and understand*. If your details
are missing, unclear, or different in different places, the assistant skips you —
not because you are bad, but because it cannot describe you clearly.

Work through this list top to bottom. Tick each box only when it is really done.

## 1. Google Business Profile — fill it fully

- [ ] Business name is your real name, spelled the same everywhere.
- [ ] Correct category (for example "Dental Clinic", not just "Clinic").
- [ ] Full address, correct pin code, and your location shown on the map.
- [ ] Phone number that someone actually answers.
- [ ] Website link, if you have one.
- [ ] Opening hours are correct, including Sundays and holidays.
- [ ] "About" text in plain words: what you do, who it is for, and which area
      you serve. Example: "We are a dental clinic in Velachery, Chennai. We do
      root canals, implants, and braces for adults and children."
- [ ] At least 10 real photos of your place, your work, and your team — with
      simple captions saying what each photo shows.

## 2. Services and areas — say them in plain words

- [ ] Your website and profile list every service you offer, each in plain words
      a customer would use. ("Root canal treatment", not "endodontic therapy".)
- [ ] Your area is named clearly: neighbourhood, city, and nearby landmarks.
- [ ] Each service names the area it covers. Example: "Bridal makeup in T. Nagar,
      Chennai" — not just "bridal makeup".

## 3. FAQ page — answer real customer questions

- [ ] Your website has a page that answers the questions customers actually ask:
      prices, timings, how long a treatment takes, what to bring, how to book.
- [ ] Each answer is short (2–4 sentences) and uses the same plain words
      customers use.
- [ ] Part 3 of this kit gives you a ready-made FAQ template.

## 4. Reviews — ask for specifics, never fake them

- [ ] After a happy customer, ask: "Could you leave a review saying which service
      you had and where?" A review that says "great root canal in Velachery"
      helps far more than one that just says "good".
- [ ] Reply to reviews, good and bad, politely and briefly.
- [ ] **Never write your own reviews. Never buy fake reviews. Never ask friends
      who were never customers to review you.** Fake reviews get removed, and
      they destroy trust when found out. Only real customers, only true words.

## 5. Same details everywhere

- [ ] Your business name, address, and phone number are spelled *exactly* the
      same on Google, Instagram, Facebook, JustDial, Practo, your website, and
      anywhere else you are listed.
- [ ] Old addresses and old phone numbers are removed or corrected wherever
      you can edit them.
- [ ] Why this matters: when different places show different details, an
      assistant cannot tell which one is true, so it picks a business whose
      details agree everywhere instead.

## 6. What NOT to do

- [ ] Do not stuff keywords into your name. ("Best Dental Clinic Chennai Cheap"
      as a business name looks fake and can get your profile suspended.)
- [ ] Do not copy a competitor's text. Write your own words.
- [ ] Do not pay anyone who promises "we will make ChatGPT rank you #1". Nobody
      can promise that — not us, not anyone. If someone guarantees it, walk away.

## When you are done

Re-run the test from Part 1 after **2–4 weeks**. Changes take time to spread
across the web, and no exact timeline can be promised. If you are named more
often than before, the fixes are working. If not, check Part 4 — it tells you
what to look at first.
