# Part 4 — How to Read Your Result

## Step 1 — Write down what happened

After running the test in Part 1 (twice, on two different days, in two assistants),
you will land in one of three places. Find yours below.

---

## Result A — You ARE named

**What it means:** the assistant already finds you and can describe you. Good —
your basics are working.

**What to fix first:** nothing urgent. Do this instead:
1. Check *what* it says about you. Is the service right? Is the area right? Is
   the phone number right? If any detail is wrong, fix it at the source (your
   Google profile or website) — wrong details send customers to the wrong place.
2. Keep doing Part 2, especially reviews. Being named once does not mean being
   named always — answers change day to day, so test again next month.
3. Do not pay anyone to "protect your ranking". There is no ranking to protect.

## Result B — You are NOT named, and nobody you know is named either

**What it means:** in your area and service, the assistants are not naming small
businesses much at all yet. They may give general advice instead of names, or
name only big chains and hospitals.

**What to fix first:**
1. Do Part 2 completely — profile, services in plain words, FAQ page, same
   details everywhere. In a quiet area, the first business with clear, complete
   details has the best chance of being picked up first.
2. Test again after 2–4 weeks. No timeline is promised — this is a "check again
   later" step, not a guarantee.
3. Do not panic and do not buy shortcuts. If the assistants are not naming
   anyone local, your customers are probably still finding you the old way
   (Google Maps, word of mouth, Instagram). Keep those strong too.

## Result C — You are NOT named, but competitors ARE

**What it means:** this is the most useful result. The assistants *do* name
businesses in your area — just not yours. Your competitors are doing something
you are not, and you can see exactly who they are.

**What to fix first:**
1. Write down the competitor names. Open their Google profiles and websites.
   Look for what they have that you don't: more reviews? A fuller profile?
   Services listed in plain words? An FAQ page? Photos with captions?
2. Fix the biggest gap first — usually it is one of these three:
   - **Your profile is half-empty** while theirs is full → finish Part 2, section 1.
   - **They have many reviews mentioning the service and area** while yours say
     nothing specific → ask happy customers for specific reviews (Part 2,
     section 4). Never fake them.
   - **Your details disagree across the web** (old phone here, new phone there)
     while theirs agree everywhere → fix section 5 of Part 2.
3. Re-test after 2–4 weeks. If a competitor is still named and you are not,
   repeat step 1 — the gap that remains is your next fix.

---

## Three truths to remember

1. **One test is not a verdict.** Always test twice, on two days, in two
   assistants, before deciding anything.
2. **Being named is not the same as getting customers.** A customer still has to
   call you, like your price, and trust you. This kit fixes the "being found"
   part only.
3. **No result here is permanent.** Assistants change their answers as the web
   changes. Re-check once a month — it takes 10 minutes.

## Where this kit's data is thin (honest note)

This kit is built from a small set of checks run in October 2026 in Chennai, on
photographers, dental clinics, skin clinics, and bridal makeup artists. We have
not tested every city, every service, or every assistant in depth. If your area
or service behaves differently, trust your own test results over anything written
here — that is why Part 1 comes first.
