# Part 1 — The Test Questions

## What this kit is

This kit helps a small business check whether AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)
mention it when a customer asks for a recommendation — and fix the simple things that
make it easier to be mentioned.

**The honest promise:** this kit improves your *chance* of being named. It never
guarantees it. Nobody can guarantee what an AI assistant will say. AI answers change
from day to day, and they are built from many sources we do not control.

**Where this kit comes from:** it is built from real checks run in October 2026 on
small businesses in Chennai (wedding photographers, dental clinics, skin clinics,
bridal makeup artists). In one of those checks, only 3 out of 20 businesses were
named. That is a small sample from one city — your area may be different. That is
exactly why Part 1 exists: test it yourself instead of believing anyone's claim.

## How to run the test (10 minutes)

1. Open a **fresh chat** in ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini. Do not use a chat where
   you already talked about your own business — a fresh chat is fairer.
2. Copy one question from below. Replace the words in [square brackets] with your
   own service and area.
3. Add this line at the end of every question: **"Don't make up names, use current
   web results."** This stops the assistant inventing businesses that do not exist.
4. Write down the exact names it gives you, and today's date.
5. **Repeat on a different day.** Ask the same question again in a fresh chat. AI
   answers change, so one test is not enough. Two tests on two days tell you much
   more than one.
6. Test in **at least two assistants** (for example ChatGPT and Perplexity). A
   business can be named in one and invisible in another.

## The questions

### Generic questions (any business)

- "Best [service] near me? Give me your top 5 names, don't make up names, use
  current web results."
- "Which [service] do you recommend in [area]? Top 5, don't make up names, use
  current web results."
- "I need a [service] in [area]. Who should I contact? Give me names, phone
  numbers, and why you picked them. Don't make up names, use current web results."

### Local-service questions (shops, clinics, studios, services in one area)

- "Best [service] in [area]? Top 5, don't make up names, use current web results."
- "Best [service] in [area] for [specific job]? Give me your top 5 names, don't
  make up names, use current web results."
- "Who is the best [service] near [landmark or neighbourhood]? Top 5, don't make
  up names, use current web results."

### One rule for the whole test

Write down what the assistant *actually* said — not what you hoped it would say.
If your business is not there, that is your real result, and Part 4 tells you what
to do about it.
