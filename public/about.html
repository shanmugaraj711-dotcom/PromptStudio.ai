About DevOps Kit

Last updated: 27 November 2026

DevOps Kit is a hands-on learning site for people who want to learn DevOps, Linux, Git, databases and cloud skills by doing — not just reading.

How it works

Every lesson comes with a practice terminal that runs inside your browser. You type real commands and see realistic output. Nothing is installed on your computer, and no servers are created for you, so there is nothing that can break and no cloud bill.

Who it is for

Students, career-switchers, support engineers and developers who want to move into DevOps. Lessons are written in plain English, and the site is available in Hindi, Telugu and Tamil too.

Free and paid

Several lessons and simulations are completely free and need no sign-in. The full course is a one-time payment — there is no subscription.

Safe by design

  • All data used in the labs is fake.
  • No advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • The practice terminals never run code on your device beyond the web page itself.
