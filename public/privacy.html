Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This page explains, in plain words, what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and name if you create an account or sign in with Google.
  • Learning progress — which lessons and simulation steps you completed, so you can continue later.
  • Purchase records — which course phase you bought and the payment reference. Card and UPI details are handled by our payment provider (Razorpay) and are never stored by us.

What we store in your browser

Your language choice and simple preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

Services we rely on

  • Firebase (Google) — sign-in and account storage.
  • Razorpay — payments, loaded only when you press a Buy button.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads.
  • The practice terminals run entirely in your browser; commands you type there are not sent to us.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and progress at any time by emailing support@promptstudioai.in.
