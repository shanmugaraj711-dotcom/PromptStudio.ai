About this simulation

This is the practice version of the lesson. Everything runs in your browser tab — no install, no server, works on your phone.

What it is: Every server in the world runs Linux. Here you practise the 12 commands you will use every single day: look around, make folders, create and copy files, and clean up. Nothing can break — this is a safe practice terminal in your browser.

What it does NOT do: it does not touch your computer or run any real service. It teaches the commands; to build the habit, repeat them on your own machine.

Your proof: Save the final ls output. It proves you can move around and manage files on a Linux machine.
