About this simulation

Practice version of the Portfolio Projects — Proof, Not Just Notes lab. It mimics the lab’s real outputs in your browser, so you can practise the commands on your phone. Nothing real runs or installs here.

The real lab: A portfolio is proof, not notes: running things an interviewer can check. This practice terminal walks through the three proof projects — a pipeline, infrastructure as code, and monitoring — and checks each one works.

What it does NOT do: it does not run a live service, it does not hold real data, and it does not touch your machine beyond this tab.

Your proof: Save the three proofs: the commit that added the pipeline, the plan that adds 2 things, and the monitoring answer with its targets up.

After you finish: run the real lab with Docker on your computer — the same commands, on your own machine. That is where the learning sticks.
