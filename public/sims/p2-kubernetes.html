About this simulation

Practice version of the Kubernetes Lab — Your App on a Small Local Cluster lab. It mimics the lab’s real outputs in your browser, so you can practise the commands on your phone. Nothing real runs or installs here.

The real lab: Kubernetes is a manager that keeps the copies of your app running — if one copy crashes, it starts a new one by itself. This practice terminal starts a small cluster on your own machine and puts your website on it.

What it does NOT do: it does not run a live service, it does not hold real data, and it does not touch your machine beyond this tab.

Your proof: Save the last get pods output: three my-site copies on version 2.0 and the fixed web-app pod, all 1/1 Running. It proves the cluster scales, rolls out a new version, and heals itself.

After you finish: run the real lab with Docker on your computer — the same commands, on your own machine. That is where the learning sticks.
