About this simulation

Practice version of the Best Sites — What To Pick, And Why lab. It mimics the lab’s real outputs in your browser, so you can practise the commands on your phone. Nothing real runs or installs here.

The real lab: Every tool has a built-in help book — you never have to memorise every flag. This practice terminal practises asking four tools for their own help, so you know where to look next time.

What it does NOT do: it does not run a live service, it does not hold real data, and it does not touch your machine beyond this tab.

Your proof: Save the four help screens. Next time you forget a command, you know the one line that shows you how.

After you finish: run the real lab with Docker on your computer — the same commands, on your own machine. That is where the learning sticks.
